### 2026-08-27 — Pivot to a digital toolkit edition

Removed the scenario simulator and rebuilt the app around the toolkit's own four-phase
process, with a participation checklist as the primary surface.

  - Deleted the AI tiers and WebLLM, the 182-template response engine, predict-before-
    reveal, the scenario model, Dexie, the Explore tab and the scenario-derived
    Communicate views. Dropped @mlc-ai/web-llm, dexie and recharts; React is the only
    runtime dependency left.
  - Added chapter 4, which had never been in the app: all seven engagement tools with
    their implementation stages, requirements, prerequisites, practical considerations,
    examples, evidence and boxes, plus the Step 4 objective-to-tool mapping.
  - Rebuilt chapter 3 and chapter 5 from the publication. An audit found that most
    content had been paraphrased or authored rather than quoted: the old profiles
    condensed the good practices and carried invented simulation content, and the four
    dissemination and glossary files had almost no basis in the publication and were
    removed with the components that rendered them.
  - Added scripts/verify-toolkit-quotes.mjs (npm run verify:quotes): every string of five
    words or more in the data files must appear in docs/toolkit/. It runs in CI and
    caught a transcription error in the new chapter 4 data.
  - New checklist derived from the tool prerequisites and requirements, the stakeholder
    good practices and the six decision framework steps. It contains no text of its own.
    State in localStorage, with JSON export and import and a print stylesheet.
  - base is now './'; the Google Fonts import is gone. dist 6.6 MB -> 301 kB,
    build 75 s -> 15 s.
  - 47 stale spec files replaced by 3. CI now gates on typecheck, verify:quotes, build
    and the e2e suite.
  - Docs for removed features moved to docs/archive/; CLAUDE.md, README.md,
    docs/README.md and docs/KNOWN-GAPS.md rewritten against the code.

Next: chapters 7 and 2 are the cheapest remaining content wins (docs/KNOWN-GAPS.md 1).
Decide whether regional-bodies and development-partners stay (KNOWN-GAPS 2).
